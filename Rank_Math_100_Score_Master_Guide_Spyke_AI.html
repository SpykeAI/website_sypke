Part 4: Critical 2026 Google Ranking Factors (Beyond Rank Math)

1. Information Gain (Google Patent)

Google penalizes articles that simply paraphrase the top 10 search results. Always include unique data, proprietary frameworks (e.g., The Spyke AI 3-Tier Call Triaging Method), original benchmark statistics, or custom case studies.

2. E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)

Each post must have a real author profile schema with author credentials and links to LinkedIn/Twitter. Use experience-based phrasing: "In our testing across 15,000 inbound contractor calls..." or "When we deployed this automated workflow...".

3. AI Overview / SGE Snippet Optimization

Place a direct 40–55 word direct answer block immediately following each main H2 question. Google AI Overviews scrape these concise answer definitions directly for featured snippets.

4. JSON-LD Schema Markup

Output rich structured data in the HTML <head>: BlogPosting Schema, FAQPage Schema (enables rich Google FAQ accordion dropdowns), and Organization/Person Schema.

5. Topical Authority Clusters (Hub & Spoke Model)

Do not publish isolated articles. Use a Pillar Page (/ai-voice-agents) linked bidirectionally to focused Spoke articles (HVAC, Real Estate, Telephony). Interlinking passes PageRank and builds site-wide topical dominance.
