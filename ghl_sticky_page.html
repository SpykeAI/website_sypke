FAQ

Questions HVAC owners ask

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and answers in real time. If asked, it says it's your virtual assistant and can transfer the caller to a person.

Does it replace my CSRs or dispatcher?

No. It covers what your team can't: after hours, weekends, lunch breaks and busy days.

What about emergency calls?

You set the rules. No-heat, no-cool, gas smell or water leak calls go straight to your on-call tech.

Does it work with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro or Jobber?

It books into your calendar. Tell us what you use on the call and we'll confirm how it connects.

What if the AI says something wrong?

You approve its script and answers before launch. Every call is recorded and transcribed, so you can check any call, and we fix issues fast.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term contract. Stay because it books you jobs, not because you signed something.

What does setup cost?

There's a one-time setup fee. It covers building, training and testing every agent. You'll get the exact number on your 1:1 call.

Can the AI use my own phone number?

Yes, through call forwarding. This depends on approval from your phone carrier and may add extra charges.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your call volume. You'll get a clear price on the 1:1 call, backed by the 90-day guarantee.
